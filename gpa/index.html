GPA Calculation Rule — Ilia State University

  1. GPA is the sum of the products of the GP (Grade Point) obtained in each course and that course's credits, divided by the total credits taken:
    GPA = Σ (GP of each course × credits of each course) / Σ (credits of each course)
  2. GP represents a course assessment converted onto a 4‑point scale.
  3. Grading categories: F 0–50 (fail), E 51–60, D 61–70, C 71–80, B 81–90, A 91–95, A+ 96–100.
  4. Within the GP system: category E (51–60) is fixed at 1.00 GP; categories D, C, B, A (61–95 points) rise in equal steps of 1/12 (≈0.08) per point; category A+ (96–100) is fixed at 4.00 GP.
  5. GPA is issued for all academic programmes except doctoral studies, and is not issued for certificate programmes.
  6. Only the final GPA is shown on the diploma supplement (except for the single‑cycle graduate medical programme).
  7. The final GPA is rounded to the nearest tenth.
